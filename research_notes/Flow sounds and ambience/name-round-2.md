# A name for the flow writer, round 2 (as of 2026-10-28)

Follows `name.md` (round 1) and `hypha-trademark.md`. Hypha is out (live HYPHA word marks in class 9, US and EU). Raphe is out ("sounds stupid": its pronunciation is split and it names the seam of the groin). So in this round **sound counts as much as meaning**.

The app: a small floating box summoned by a hotkey. You type with your eyes on one spot while the text glides left under a fixed caret, one click per key, nothing to react to; ⌘⏎ pastes the text into the app behind. Siblings: the terminal app and the brief app, short biology words used as plain names.

Criteria, in order:
1. **Sound.** Good aloud in English and German: soft consonants, open vowels, 1–2 syllables, at most 7 letters, no awkward cluster, no medical or anatomical embarrassment, no joke in either language.
2. **Meaning.** Calm, unbroken attention, flow that only goes forward; preferably biology, in the terminal app's family.
3. **Registers.** No live word mark in Nice classes 9 or 42 (DE/EU/IR and US), no writing, notes or focus app of that name, no well-known software product; ideally `<name>.app` free.

A second family was added on request: **short English compounds** (Flowtype, Typeflow, Typeline …), screened the same way (§2, §5).

This is a research note, not legal advice. A clearance search before a public release is a lawyer's job.

## Takeaway

- **Top 3: 1 Lemna, 2 Naiad, 3 Stolo** (§7). Lemna sounds best and has the cleanest record. Naiad means best ("the flowing one"), but its pronunciation varies and a calm river-nymph video game holds an EU class 9 mark. Stolo (the plant's runner, growing forward along one line) is clean in the registers, but a new iOS messenger uses the name.
- **A biology word is the stronger choice than a descriptive compound.** Every obvious compound is already a Mac typing or writing tool. **FlowType** is a menu-bar app that plays sounds as you type, which is this app's click. **CalmType** is a Mac text pad for writing before you send. Then **TypeFlow** (AI keyboard), **Stillwrite** (Markdown editor), **Flowwrite** (focus writing), **Typeglide** (AI writing in text fields) and **Calmtype** (a Mac journal, "a calm place to write"). **Flowtype is also Meta's Flow**, the JavaScript type checker first released at flowtype.org (§5). The compounds that are still free (Lineglide, Typeline, Lineflow) are free because they only describe. That makes them weak marks, and they leave the the terminal app/the brief app family.
- **.app is gone for nearly every real word.** Of 62 names pre-screened, `.app` is free only for Lotica and for compounds (Lineglide, Slowtype, Typewake, Glidewrite, Glidetype, Stillwrite, Calmtype, Calmline, Stilltype, Typestill). Every biology word on the shortlist needs a variant: `<name>app.com`, `get<name>.com` or `<name>writer.app`, all free for the top 3.
- Words that sounded right but are held in software: Velum (sewts GmbH: EU word mark in 9/42, US in 7/9), Limen (four EU word marks in 9 or 42), Tela (a Mac notes app named Tela, plus EU TELA in class 9/42 held by GeoQuest Systems, an SLB company as far as I know), Tilia (Tilia GmbH and Tilia Inc., class 9/42), Lumen, Vela, Pia, Nema, Seta, Hyla, Alula, Calla, Kyma. Flumen is held by use: an iOS eBook reader and an open-source macOS focus app are both called Flumen.

## Method (all on 2026-10-28)

- **DE/EU/IR marks: DPMAregister.** Its smart search answers as JSON at `https://register.dpma.de/DPMAregister/smartsearch?queryString=<word>`. Its link opens the hit list, which can be downloaded as CSV. The CSV header shows the query actually run: `(rn OR akz) OR marke="<word>" OR (inh OR anm)="<word>"`. So it searches **mark wording and owner/applicant names, as whole words, not the goods text** (this corrects the description of the smart search in `hypha-trademark.md`). For every hit that was not dead, I fetched the detail page and read the classes, owner, status and class 9/42 goods. The control query "hypha" found the same four EU marks as before. Limits: whole words only (`typeflow` does not find TYPE FLOW). Figurative marks do not show their word element, so a figurative hit in 9/42 is listed as "figurative" and was not opened as an image. Hit counts include owner-name matches (people called Liane, companies called Velum). 58 queries and 877 detail pages, about 1000 requests in all, well under the DPMA limit of 5000 a day.
- **US marks: Trademarkia's search page** (`https://www.trademarkia.com/search/trademarks?q=<word>`), the mirror `hypha-trademark.md` used, read through a fetch tool. The page lists 10 hits, so further pages were read where it mattered (Naiad 3 of 3, Hyla 3 of 4). Status as shown there, **not verified at USPTO TSDR**. Two-word queries (`slow type`) return OR matches and are useless.
- **Apps:** Apple's iTunes Search API, Mac (`entity=macSoftware`) and iOS (`entity=software`), **US and DE storefronts**, counting titles that contain the word (substrings included, so "Pinnacle" counts for Pinna).
- **GitHub:** public search API, `<word> in:name`, sorted by stars.
- **Domains:** RDAP through `https://rdap.org/domain/<name>`. When rdap.org rate-limited, I went to the registries directly (Google Registry for .app/.dev, Verisign for .com). Taken = registered, with the registration date. For some I fetched the site to see who uses it.
- **Pronunciation:** my reading of how English and German speakers say the written word; **not tested with speakers**.
- Not reached or not tried: USPTO TSDR, EUIPO eSearch plus, WIPO Global Brand Database, UK IPO, the Swiss register. Not checked: domain prices, social handles, Setapp.

## 7. Top 3 (sound weighed as heavily as meaning)

1. **Lemna.** The best sound of the round: one pronunciation in both languages, soft l-m-n, two syllables, ending in -a like the terminal app. The cleanest record of any real word: no live mark in 9/42 in DE, EU or US, no app of the name, no writing or focus product. It also gives the simplest icon, the existing line with one frond and its root as the caret. the terminal app · the brief app · Lemna stay one family of plain scientific names. **Cost:** the meaning is calm surface, not forward flow; "duckweed" in English; lemna.app is taken (lemnaapp.com, getlemna.com, lemnawriter.app are free); Lemna Bio is a software namesake in AI biotech.
2. **Naiad.** The best meaning: "the flowing one", and the larva that lives under water before it flies. A soft sound in every variant. **Cost:** people will say it differently (NY-ad, NAY-ad, German *Najade*), and the calm river-nymph game NAIAD holds an EU class 9 figurative mark. Video games and a writing app are different goods, but both are class 9 software, and the game shares the mood. Medium risk; a lawyer's question before a release.
3. **Stolo.** A line that only grows forward and leaves rooted what it laid: the writing surface in one plant. One pronunciation, two syllables, a clean register. **Cost:** a new iOS messenger, Stolo Systems, uses the name (unregistered as far as found), and the *st-* makes it less soft than Lemna. stolo.app is taken.

**Biology word or descriptive compound?** The biology word is the stronger choice.
- **The compounds are where the crowd already is.** Every obvious one names a Mac typing or writing tool: FlowType (typing sounds in the menu bar), CalmType (a text pad before you send), TypeFlow, Stillwrite, Flowwrite, Typeglide, Calmtype. Flowtype is also Meta's Flow.
- **The few that are free are free because they only describe.** That makes them weak marks: an EU or German application risks refusal as descriptive, and anyone can name a product next to them (Lineglide sits beside live GLIDE software marks).
- **Recognition buys little here.** A hotkey app is seldom seen by name (round 1, §6).
- **The biology word keeps the family.** the terminal app · the brief app · Lemna.

If the user prefers a name that says what it does, **Lineglide** is the one to take: clean registers, `.app` free, exact. It reads as a feature description rather than a name, and it gives up the sibling family.

Before committing to any of them: a TSDR and EUIPO check in 9 and 42, and a UK check. For Lemna, also the Swiss register (Lemna Bio). For Naiad, the full goods of EM 018665022 (open the record and its image). For Stolo, whether Stolo Systems has filed a mark.
