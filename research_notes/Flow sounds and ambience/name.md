# A name for the flow writer (working name "Flow"), as of 2026-10-28

Scope: a name for the small floating box summoned by a hotkey (one line, caret fixed, text glides left, one click per key, ⌘⏎ pastes into the app behind). Wanted: short (1–2 syllables, ≤ 8 letters), about flow, calm or unbroken attention, preferably a biology word used plain, like its siblings the terminal app (Greek "glue", the class of non-neuronal brain cells) and the brief app (a glial cell type: specialised ependymal glia lining the third ventricle, with one long process and no beating cilia; [Wikipedia: the brief app](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/the brief app)).

Method (all on 2026-10-28):
- **Domains:** registry RDAP, not WHOIS: Google Registry (`pubapi.registry.google/rdap`) for .app and .dev, Verisign (`rdap.verisign.com`) for .com, PIR for .org. 200 = registered, 404 = not registered that day. "Registered" says nothing about use; "free" says nothing about premium pricing. For some registered .app names the site was fetched to see who holds it.
- **App Store:** Apple's public iTunes Search API, US store, `entity=macSoftware` and `entity=software` (iOS), counted only apps whose title contains the word. Other storefronts not checked.
- **GitHub:** public search API, repositories with the word in the name (`in:name`), total count and top three by stars. Counts include noise (surnames, substrings).
- **Trademarks:** USPTO and EUIPO were **not** queried directly (the USPTO search API and Justia's pages refuse automated access); what is listed comes from web search over aggregators (Justia Trademarks, uspto.report, TrademarkElite, GleanMark) and is **unverified**: status may be stale, and absence of a hit is not clearance. DPMA (Germany) not searched.
- **Pronunciation in German:** my reading of how a German speaker says the written word; not tested with speakers.

## Takeaway

- Every plain single word in this field is held somewhere; the question is only *where*. Words already used in the writing/focus/reading space are out (§3): Flow, Stream, Focus, Zen, Calm, Still, Drift, Hush, Glide, Saccade, Fovea.
- **Recommended: 1 Hypha, 2 Ductus, 3 Raphe.** Hypha is the closest picture of the surface (a hypha grows only at its tip; everything behind it stays where it was laid) and the closest sibling in sound and kind to the terminal app (Greek, cell-scale, two syllables ending in -a); its conflicts are outside writing. Ductus is the word for the flow of handwriting (German *Duktus* is a living word for exactly that), with the cleanest conflict record of the strong candidates. Raphe joins gliding (diatoms glide on their raphe) and calm (the raphe nuclei make the brain's serotonin), and is the only strong candidate whose .app is free; its pronunciation is split.
- **Lotic** (of flowing water) is the best-sounding calm word, but LOTIC.AI LLC filed LOTIC in the US for downloadable app software (2022, status unverified). If that filing is dead, Lotic is a fair swap for Raphe.
- **Fovea** fits best of all (the eye's fixed spot) and is not usable: two Mac apps and an iOS speed-reading app built on the same fixed-gaze idea already carry it.
- **Schwann** is the only candidate that is literally another glial cell (the terminal app · the brief app · Schwann) and in German reads as "swan" (gliding); in German-speaking markets it reads as the pen maker Schwan-STABILO.
- Mindfulness words (sati, mushin, zazen, shoshin, dhyana, shunya, samu, ma) are all taken by meditation apps, and using them for a product falls under the published critique of commodified Buddhist practice; *sati* also names widow-burning in Hindi. Not recommended.

### Hypha (2 syllables, 5 letters)
- **Fit:** a fungal hypha grows at its tip only: new wall is assembled at the apex, fed by the Spitzenkörper, a "vesicle supply centre" ([Deacon, Edinburgh](https://archive.bio.ed.ac.uk/jdeacon/microbes/apical.htm); [PMC23049](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC23049)). The line does the same: text emerges at the caret and everything behind stays as laid. From Greek *hyphē* "web", from *hyphainein* "to weave" ([etymonline](https://www.etymonline.com/word/hypha), [Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/hypha)); "text" is from Latin *texere*, also "to weave" (standard etymology, not re-checked here). Mycelium: quiet, unbroken, below the surface.
- **Say:** EN "HY-fuh". DE: the German word is *Hyphe* ("HÜ-fe"), so German readers may say "HÜ-fa". Looks like "hyphen" (a writing mark; harmless, maybe a plus).
- **App Store:** no Mac app. iOS: "Hypha: Grow Mushrooms" (Demeter Mycolabs), "Hypha Wallet" (Hypha Limited, a DAO wallet, Productivity), "DanceHypha", "Hyphae Learning".
- **Software and products:** Hypha, Open Technology Fund's open-source grant/submission management platform (BSD-3; hypha.app → docs.hypha.app; [OTF](https://www.opentech.fund/news/otf-conducts-security-audit-of-hypha/)); Hypha Worker Co-operative, Toronto ([GitHub](https://github.com/hyphacoop)); Hypha, a London innovation-intelligence platform ([Built In](https://builtin.com/company/hypha)); Hypha DAO.
- **GitHub:** 559 repos; CodeSoul-co/Hypha 449★ (LLM agent framework), Hypha-ROS race cars.
- **Domains:** hypha.app (since 2019, OTF), hypha.com, hypha.dev, hypha.org, hyphaapp.com, gethypha.com all taken; **hyphawriter.app free**.
- **Trademarks:** US HYPHA reg. 6135016, music-group services, an individual ([Justia](https://trademarks.justia.com/887/50/hypha-88750915.html)); HYPHA LABS (bioreactors); HYPHA LITE (EU, leather, class 18); HYPHAE (materials-science software, intent-to-use, [uspto.report](https://uspto.report/TM/99339039)). None found in writing or text software.
- **Other associations:** mushrooms (also home psilocybin growing, where "hyphae" is common vocabulary), crypto DAO.
- **Verdict:** recommended. Best picture of the surface, best family fit; conflicts sit in grants, crypto and mushrooms, not writing.

### Ductus (2 syllables, 6 letters)
- **Fit:** in palaeography the *ductus* is the number, order, direction and speed of the strokes that form letters ([HMML lexicon](https://hmmlschool.org/lexicon/14517/); [Vatican Latin paleography](https://spotlight.vatlib.it/latin-paleography/feature/1-some-important-premises)); German *Duktus* is the characteristic stroke of a hand or the flow of someone's speech and writing, from Latin *ductus* "leading, conducting" ([Duden](https://www.duden.de/rechtschreibung/Duktus); [verbformen](https://www.verbformen.com/declension/nouns/Duktus.htm)). Anatomy: a duct carries flow one way (ductus deferens, ductus arteriosus, ductus cochlearis). The caret leads, the line follows.
- **Say:** EN "DUK-tus" (an English ear may hear "duck"); DE "DUK-tus", a known word.
- **App Store:** none for Ductus or Duktus (Mac or iOS).
- **Software and products:** Data Ductus (Swedish IT consultancy; small repos under `data-ductus`); wikiotics/ductus (retired wiki); Ductus, an Australian HVAC firm filing ducting marks ([TrademarkElite](https://www.trademarkelite.com/australia/trademark/trademark-detail/2674269/R-REGAL-RR-REGAL-ROUND-RS-REGAL-SHIELD)); many HVAC "duct" apps.
- **GitHub:** 80 repos, top 4★.
- **Domains:** ductus.app (registered 2024-09-20, no HTTPS response), ductus.com, ductus.dev, ductus.org, getductus.com taken; **ductusapp.com and ductuswriter.app free**. duktus.app registered 2026-04-20 (a page titled "Duktus"); **duktusapp.com, getduktus.com free**.
- **Trademarks:** no standalone DUCTUS found in US aggregators, only fraternity mottos containing the word ([Justia](https://trademarks.justia.com/975/03/unus-probitas-omega-delta-phi-integritas-97503885.html)). EUIPO/DPMA unchecked.
- **Other associations:** HVAC, duct tape; patent ductus arteriosus (a newborn heart condition); ductus deferens.
- **Verdict:** recommended. The only candidate whose ordinary meaning *is* the flow of writing; cleanest record; hard sound and plumbing overtones.

### Raphe (2 syllables, 5 letters)
- **Fit:** three readings, all biology. (1) Pennate diatoms glide over surfaces with no appendages, pushing mucilage strands out through the raphe, a slit in their silica wall ([PNAS 2025, PMC11962607](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11962607/); [TU Dresden](https://tu-dresden.de/cmcb/bcube/forschungsgruppen/kroeger/research/biological-adhesion-and-motility)): a cell that glides on its seam. (2) The raphe nuclei are the brain's main source of serotonin ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raphe_nuclei); [StatPearls](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31335079/)): calm, mood. (3) Greek *rhaphē* "seam": words joined into one line.
- **Say:** EN split: "RAY-fee" ([Forvo](https://forvo.com/word/raphe/)) vs "RAF-ee" ([pronounce.tv](https://pronounce.tv/word/raphe)); many will read "rafe". DE "RA-fe". Sound-alikes: Rapha (cycling brand), Raphael.
- **App Store:** none (Mac or iOS).
- **Software and products:** none found under the exact name; Raphael AI at raphael.app ([Krowdbase](https://www.krowdbase.com/software/raphael-ai)).
- **GitHub:** 253, all "Rapheal…" names, none notable.
- **Domains:** **raphe.app, rapheapp.com, getraphe.com, raphewriter.app free**; raphe.com, raphe.dev (2025-03-25) taken.
- **Trademarks:** US "RAPHE PHARMACEUTIQUE LABORATORIE" (2023, OTC drug manufacture, an individual), per aggregator snippet only.
- **Other associations:** anatomy textbooks use "median raphe" for the seam of the perineum, scrotum, palate and tongue.
- **Verdict:** recommended third. Best availability of any strong name; the pronunciation split is the real cost.

### Kinesin (3 syllables, 7 letters)
- **Fit:** kinesin-1 walks hand over hand toward the microtubule's plus end, one 8 nm step per ATP, about 100 steps per run; it is the anterograde motor that carries cargo down the axon toward the synapse ([Yildiz et al., PMC1472431](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1472431); [Asbury 2003](https://faculty.washington.edu/casbury/pdfs/Asbury%202003%20Science.pdf); [review, arXiv 1507.06166](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1507.06166)). One key, one step, one click, one direction; the cargo is delivered at the end (the paste).
- **Say:** EN "kih-NEE-sin"; DE "ki-ne-ZIN".
- **App Store:** none.
- **Software and products:** kinesin.app hosts "Kinesin – The world, one chapter at a time", an illustrated serial-story reading app in private preview, launch "mid-November '26" (page fetched 2026-10-28); Kinesin Limited, Irish practice-management software ([Enterprise Ireland](https://directory.enterprise-ireland.com/vendor/life-sciences-health-tech/kinesin-limited)). Near-names: Kinesis ergonomic keyboards (since 1992; "a registered trademark" per a 2003 manual, [ErgoCanada PDF](https://ergocanada.com/products/keyboards/kinesis_advantage_usb.pdf); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinesis_(keyboard))), i.e. typing hardware; Amazon Kinesis.
- **GitHub:** 62 (KinesinLMS 6★).
- **Domains:** kinesin.app (2026-07-30), .com, .dev, .org taken; **kinesinapp.com, getkinesin.com, kinesinwriter.app free**.
- **Trademarks:** none found.
- **Verdict:** shortlist. The best match for the mechanical click; not calm, three syllables, a consumer reading app with the exact name launches next month, and Kinesis is one letter away in keyboards.

## 6. How the name will show up

- **Menu-bar item:** a glyph only, no text (an accessory app, no Dock icon). It is a template image (monochrome, adapts to light/dark menu bars). The current glyph, a short line with a caret bar, works for any name; a name-specific motif must stay legible at menu-bar size: Hypha's line-and-tip-dot is nearly the current glyph; Ductus's tapered stroke also; Raphe's ellipse with a slit is the most distinct at small size.
- **Where the word is read:** the menu's items ("Show <Name>  ⌃⌥Space", "Open Window", "Discard…"); the Accessibility prompt and the list in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility (name + icon; the name must be recognisable there, since paste needs it); Login Items; the window title in window mode; Activity Monitor. A hotkey app is seldom seen by name, so recognisability in those lists matters more than a long story behind the word.
- **Bundle identifier:** the siblings use `dev.example-a.app` and `dev.example-b.app`; the placeholder here is `org.flow.app` (`app/make-app.sh`) and the keychain service `org.flow.app.session` (`lib/FlowJournal/Sources/FlowJournal/KeyStore.swift`). Following the family: `dev.<name>.app` (e.g. `dev.hypha.app`, `dev.ductus.app`, `dev.raphe.app`). Apple asks for a unique reverse-DNS string; whether Developer ID signing or notarisation checks domain ownership was not verified here (I know of no such check).
- **Cost of renaming:** a new bundle id voids the Accessibility grant (the README already notes `tccutil reset Accessibility org.flow.app`), orphans the keychain service and the journal folder (`~/Library/Application Support/Flow/…`), and changes what the self-signed dev certificate is called if it is renamed too. Rename once, after finishing or discarding open sessions, before any build goes to others. 28 Swift/shell files mention "Flow"; module names (`FlowJournal`, `FlowClicks`) are internal and need not change.
