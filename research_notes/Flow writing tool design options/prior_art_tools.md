# Prior art: forward-only writing, typing sounds, and typing into the focused app on macOS (as of 2026-10-27)

Scope: tools relevant to a macOS writer where the eye stays on one fixed spot, written text slides away (horizontal, "word wrap off" style), reacting to earlier text is hard, optional typewriter/mechanical key sounds, and output stays in the tool or is pasted into the focused app. GitHub figures (stars, licence, last push) were read from the public GitHub API on 2026-10-27; source files were read at `main` HEAD that day.

## 1. Forward-only / hidden-text writing tools: what exists, which hide text, which block backspace, which have a horizontal fixed-cursor mode?

### Takeaway
Many tools hide or punish earlier text (Ilys, The Most Dangerous Writing App, Flowstate, Write or Die, Smashkey, Blind Draft, Invisible Notes, Freewrite Alpha/AlphaSmart through small screens). Every "typewriter mode" found (iA Writer, Ulysses, Scrivener, Obsidian, FocusWriter) is vertical: the current line stays fixed and the page scrolls up. **No current writing tool was found with a horizontal single-line "cursor stays put, text slides left" mode.** The nearest precedents are hardware: 1980s electronic typewriters with a one-line LCD, and a terminal toy (DoggyWriter) showing two lines.

### Inferences
- The horizontal fixed-cursor single-line mode is genuinely unoccupied in software; prior art is 1980s typewriter LCDs (one line, text sliding as you type), which is a good name-able lineage ("the typewriter's line window") and evidence the interaction is learnable.
- Two camps exist: *punitive* (MDWA, Flowstate, Write or Die, Cold Turkey: deletion or lockout threats) and *calm* (Ilys, Smashkey, Alpha, OmmWriter). The requested "mindfulness" vibe aligns only with the calm camp; no calm tool also has sounds plus a tiny visible window except the Freewrite Alpha (hardware, $349).
- The backspace question is the main design fork: strict no-delete (Draft, Hemingway Mode, Smashkey default) draws "feels awful" complaints; the most-copied compromise is "backspace allowed within the current word/line only" (Hemingway Typewriter fork, Brother EP-44's 15-character correction window).
- Most hidden-text web tools are old (2014–2016) and unmaintained; the active open-source ones are FocusWriter (GPL-3), freewrite by Farza (MIT), Zenpen (MIT), Obsidian plugins (MIT).

## 2. Typing-sound apps for macOS: how they capture keys, latency, sound packs and their licences, per-key variation, mouse sounds, reviews

### Takeaway
Thock (MIT, Swift, active) is the best open-source model: a CGEvent tap on keyDown/keyUp/flagsChanged, random sample per key type, pitch variation, key-up sounds, mouse sounds, spatial audio and a soundpack registry where each pack declares its licence. Reusable sound licences: Mechvibes and tplai/kbsim packs are MIT, some Thock packs CC0 or Pixabay; Tickeys' Freesound packs are CC0/Sampling+; **no permissively licensed typewriter pack was found** (Tickeys' typewriter pack has no licence file; FocusWriter's typewriter sound sits in a GPL-3 repo; Kachunk's typewriter recordings are proprietary).

Setup is adding it to Accessibility; launched 2023 at $3.99; default Everglide Crystal Purple, plus Oreo, NovelKeys Cream — [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2023/04/05/klack-app-mechanical-keyboard-sound-effects-for-mac/). Cannot mute the physical keyboard — [Creative Bloq](https://creativebloq.com/news/klack-macbook-mechanical-keyboard). Milky Yellow switches and mouse click sounds added — [AlternativeTo news, Apr 2025](https://alternativeto.net/news/2025/4/klack-update-introduces-new-milky-yellow-switches).
- Kachunk: macOS 13+, Sparkle; free with free packs, $4.99 once for all 26 packs; maker "recorded two typewriters and 24 mechanical keyboard switches, with slight pitch and volume variation on each strike"; margin bell; no analytics/telemetry/account — [hunted.space / Product Hunt](https://hunted.space/product/kachunk).
- Other: KeyBell free menu-bar app — [Softpedia](https://mac.softpedia.com/get/Audio/KeyBell.shtml); Typewriter Keyboard: macOS 10.10+, Intel only — [Softpedia](https://mac.softpedia.com/get/Utilities/Typewriter-Keyboard.shtml); Loud Typer: per-application sounds — [AppleVis](https://applevis.com/comment/113970); Noisy Typer discussed for Mavericks — [MacRumors](https://forums.macrumors.com/threads/noisy-typer-for-mavericks.1692110/); TypeLouder paid/proprietary — [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/software/typelouder).
- Permission model (Apple DTS, Mar 2026): three TCC privileges — `PostEvent` (CGEvent.post), `ListenEvent` (event taps), `Accessibility`; "Both `PostEvent` and `Accessibility` map to Accessibility" in the UI while "`ListenEvent` maps to Input Monitoring"; PostEvent and ListenEvent APIs "_are_ compatible with the App Sandbox" — [Apple Developer Forums](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/820594).
- Bluetooth latency: first-gen AirPods measured 274 ms, second-gen 178 ms — [Pixel Envy linking Stephen Coyle](https://pxlnv.com/linklog/airpods-latency/).

### Inferences
- A writing app that plays sounds only for its own window needs **no** event tap or permission (it gets key events from its own window); system-wide sound apps need Input Monitoring/Accessibility. This removes the single biggest trust hurdle that Klack/Thock face.
- Over Bluetooth headphones, any click will trail the keypress by ~150–300 ms (AirPods figures above); this is outside an app's control and worth a one-line note in the UI rather than engineering.
- Sampling+ 1.0 (in Tickeys' Freesound notes) is a retired Creative Commons licence with restrictions on advertising use (background knowledge; verify before reuse); CC0 files from those packs are safe. Reuse should prefer MIT (Mechvibes, kbsim) or CC0 packs and keep attribution files.
- For a typewriter sound there is no clean permissive source among the apps surveyed; options are recording one, or sourcing CC0 recordings (e.g., Freesound) per file.

### Gaps
- No primary source pinned the claim that Klack lags over Bluetooth (seen only in a search summary).
- Licences of community packs on mechvibes.com were not found; they must be checked pack by pack.
- Klack's per-key variation behaviour and capture API (tap vs NSEvent global monitor) are not documented publicly.
- "Keyclicker" status was not established (AlternativeTo page exists: [KeyClicker](https://www.alternativeto.net/software/keyclicker/)).
- No user threads specifically saying typing sounds "got annoying" over time were found; the one related complaint is system-wide sounds firing when not writing (e.g., launching apps via Alfred) — [L&L forum](https://forum.literatureandlatte.com/t/customized-typing-sounds/15277).

## 3. "Type into the focused app" tools as a model for the output path: how they insert text and what breaks

### Takeaway
Everyone converges on the same two mechanisms: (a) put text on the pasteboard and post ⌘V via `CGEvent` (default in Wispr Flow, Handy, VoiceInk, Espanso for long text), restoring the old clipboard afterwards; (b) "direct typing" with `CGEventKeyboardSetUnicodeString` in small chunks (VoiceInk, Handy "Direct Input", Espanso Inject). Known breakages: no focused text field, paste timing (old clipboard pasted), apps/terminals/TUIs that ignore or intercept ⌘V, remote desktop/games/elevated apps that refuse synthetic input, Secure Input (password managers) blocking everything, clipboard pollution of clipboard managers, keyboard-layout mismatches, and the Mac App Store sandbox (auto-paste effectively unavailable there).

### Cited Findings
- VoiceInk (GPL-3.0 per LICENSE file; GitHub API shows NOASSERTION; Swift, 6,942★, active) offers three methods: "Default" (clipboard + ⌘V), "AppleScript", "Direct Typing" — `PasteMethod.swift` — [GitHub](https://github.com/Beingpax/VoiceInk/tree/main/VoiceInk/Infrastructure/SystemIntegration/Paste).
  - Default: checks `AXIsProcessTrusted()`, creates `CGEventSource(stateID: .privateState)`, posts ⌘-down, V-down, V-up, ⌘-up (virtual keys 0x37/0x09) to `.cghidEventTap` with 10 ms between events; `prePasteDelay` 0.20 s; clipboard restored after ≥0.25 s and only if the pasteboard still holds VoiceInk's text and session marker; marks its item with `org.nspasteboard.TransientType` so clipboard managers skip it — `CursorPaster.swift`, `ClipboardManager.swift` (same link).
  - AppleScript path: uses `key code 9 using command down` when the layout name ends in "⌘" ("X – QWERTY ⌘" layouts remap under Command, so `keystroke "v"` would hit the wrong key) — `CursorPaster.swift`.
  - Direct Typing: `keyboardSetUnicodeString` in chunks of 20 UTF-16 units ("Several macOS injectors use this conservative limit for compatibility with receivers that truncate longer Unicode event payloads"), 10 ms between chunks, sent on the space key (0x31); newline sent as Unicode CR with Shift to get a line break, never a physical Return; refuses control characters; never retries a partial send ("a retry could duplicate text"); comment: "Posting events does not establish that the receiving editor accepted them." — `DirectTextTyper.swift`.
- Wispr Flow: "Flow temporarily uses your clipboard to paste text and restores your previous contents afterward"; failures include old clipboard contents pasted and pasting working only in some apps; fixes: focus a text field first, re-grant Accessibility, "Paste last transcript" (Ctrl+⌘V), test in Notes because some apps block paste — [Wispr docs](https://docs.wisprflow.ai/articles/7971211038-fix-text-not-pasting-after-dictation). "Clipboard function not working anymore" is a recurring r/WisprFlow request theme (12 posts) — [GummySearch](https://gummysearch.com/r/WisprFlow/). After 700+ responses to a "haters" call, main complaints were the Flow Bar covering controls (Gmail send, Dock), over-aggressive Auto Cleanup changing words, outages; article dated 2026-07-29 — [Digital Trends](https://www.digitaltrends.com/computing/wispr-flow-asked-its-haters-what-was-wrong-and-more-than-700-people-answered/).
- Handy (MIT, Rust, 34,870★): macOS default is Clipboard (⌘V); Direct Input "does not account for non-US keyboard layouts"; "None" copies only; terminal-specific methods (Shift+Insert, Ctrl+Shift+V) on Windows/Linux; paste delay default 60 ms, raise to 60–200 ms if wrong text pastes; "Don't Modify Clipboard" restores previous contents — [Handy docs](https://handy.computer/docs/paste-methods), [troubleshooting](https://handy.computer/docs/troubleshooting).
- Espanso (GPL-3.0, Rust, 14,688★): current docs: `backend` "Auto … by default to automatically choose the most appropriate one"; `clipboard_threshold` = chars after which the clipboard backend is used; `inject_delay`/`key_delay` "Increase if the target application is missing some characters/key events"; `preserve_clipboard`, `restore_clipboard_delay`, `pre_paste_delay`; built-in patches "particularly terminals" — [Espanso options](https://espanso.org/docs/configuration/options/). Older docs said Inject was the macOS default and that keystroke injection had trouble with emoji/accents — [mirror of old docs](https://git.nunosempere.com/open.source/espanso/commit/a480ad1a0326e8e8c2156891e5a835a3ea8a896f) (conflict with current docs: version change). Secure Input "prevents applications from performing all accessibility-related tasks … such as a password field"; most common cause of a stuck lock is a password manager in the background — [Espanso: Secure Input](https://espanso.org/docs/troubleshooting/secure-input).
- Superwhisper: users request restoring the previous clipboard because pasting "overwrites the data in your clipboard" — [Superwhisper feedback](https://superwhisper.userjot.com/board/p/restore-the-last-value-from-the-clipboard); transcripts clutter clipboard-manager history — [feedback](https://superwhisper.userjot.com/board/p/transcripts-cluttering-up-my-clipboard-history); auto-paste did not reliably reach a TUI editor without manual Ctrl+V — [pi-superwhisper-paste](https://pi.dev/packages/pi-superwhisper-paste?page=13).
- OpenWhispr: paste failures in some apps are "the target application refusing simulated input"; examples: elevated apps, remote desktop clients, some games; on macOS needs Accessibility; stale permission entries after updates need a reset — [OpenWhispr docs](https://docs.openwhispr.com/help/fix/text-not-pasting.md).
- Mac App Store: "The Mac App Store version of Spokenly can't paste text automatically … App Store rules don't allow it to use Accessibility"; workaround: user presses ⌘V — [Spokenly docs](https://spokenly.app/docs/mac-app-store). A sandboxed clipboard manager posting ⌘V via `CGEvent.post` was rejected twice under Guideline 2.4.5 (Mar 2026; same rejection reported Sep 2026), though DTS says PostEvent is sandbox-compatible — [Apple Developer Forums](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/820594). ScreenFloat: direct-paste would need the Accessibility entitlement "which the Mac App Store does not allow" — [Eternal Storms blog](https://blog.eternalstorms.at/?p=5846). A CGEvent paste can silently do nothing; sandbox and TCC often explain it — [Apple Developer Forums](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/773989).
- Focus hand-back: a script run from a floating window can leave the target inactive so the paste lands nowhere; one fix re-clicks the saved cursor position — [MacScripter](https://www.macscripter.net/viewtopic.php?id=47638).
- AssemblyAI Blurt (MIT, Swift): "Open-source macOS dictation that turns speech into polished text in the app you're already using" — [GitHub](https://github.com/AssemblyAI/blurt).

### Inferences
- For a writer tool, the clipboard+⌘V path with transient marking and conditional restore (VoiceInk's approach) is the most robust single default; direct Unicode typing is the fallback for apps that block paste but is slow for long text and can drop characters in Electron/remote apps (inject_delay knobs exist for this reason).
- Because output happens once at the end of a session (not per keystroke), the tool should keep the text until the user confirms it landed, and fall back to "it's on the clipboard, press ⌘V" — the pattern Wispr Flow and OpenWhispr already use.
- Distributing outside the Mac App Store (Developer ID + notarisation) is effectively required for one-step paste-into-app; a MAS build would degrade to "copy, then user presses ⌘V".
- Terminals: Cmd+V works in Terminal.app/iTerm on macOS (inference, not stated by sources), but multi-line pastes trigger bracketed-paste/confirmation prompts in some terminals and TUIs; this is worth testing, not assuming.

### Gaps
- How Apple Dictation inserts text internally (text input system vs events) was not found in public sources; an InputMethodKit-based insertion path (inserting via the text input client rather than synthetic events) is plausible but no dictation app documenting it was found.
- No systematic list of macOS apps that reject synthetic ⌘V or Unicode events was found; evidence is anecdotal (TUIs, remote desktop, games, some Electron apps).
- Raycast snippets and TextExpander insertion mechanics were not researched in detail.

## 4. Focus / dimming tools: HazeOver, macOS Focus; do writers want dimming or does full screen suffice?

### Takeaway
HazeOver is the established "dim everything but the active window" tool (well rated on Setapp), with alternatives such as Hocus Focus; writing apps instead dim within the document (iA Writer, Calmly Writer, Obsidian Typewriter Mode) and iA notes focus mode "shines" in full-screen dark mode. No direct evidence was found that writers ask for system-wide dimming on top of a full-screen writing window.

### Cited Findings
- HazeOver puts "a sort of translucent curtain behind your frontmost window"; adjustable dim, fade speed, per-display, toggle shortcut — [Macworld 1.6 review (Kirkville repost)](https://kirkville.com/hazeover-1-6-review-dim-those-background-windows-so-you-can-focus-on-the-task-at-hand/). MacStories: dimming kept the reviewer working instead of checking a Twitter client behind — [MacStories](https://www.macstories.net/reviews/hazeover-lets-you-concentrate-by-fading-inactive-apps/). Setapp: 99% positive from 942 ratings, v1.9.5, macOS 11+ — [Setapp](https://setapp.com/apps/hazeover). Alternatives: Window Focus (paid), Hocus Focus (hides inactive windows after a time) — [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/software/hazeover).
- iA user feedback: focus mode "shines at best when used in full-screen, dark mode" — [iA support](https://ia.net/writer/support/editor/focus-mode).
- In-document dimming: Calmly Writer focus mode dims all but the paragraph — [Calmly FAQ](https://calmlywriter.com/help.htm); Obsidian Typewriter Mode dims other paragraphs — [Obsidian forum](https://forum.obsidian.md/t/shoutout-typewriter-mode-plugin-is-fantastic/74627).
- OmmWriter "opens up in full-screen mode, so nothing disrupts your creative process" — [ommwriter.com](https://ommwriter.com).

### Inferences
- For a fixed-eye single-line writer, a full-screen or large centred window already does what HazeOver does; dimming other windows matters only if the tool is a small floating panel over the target app (the paste-into-app mode). There, a soft scrim behind the panel is the HazeOver pattern applied locally, not a system feature.

### Gaps
- No user research or forum threads were found where writers compare HazeOver-style dimming with full-screen writing apps; Literature & Latte's "full screen vs typewriter mode" blog post exists but was not read — [L&L blog](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/blog/full-screen-vs-typewriter-mode).
- macOS Focus modes (notification filtering) were not researched in relation to writing tools.

## 5. Does any app already combine fixed-eye typing + sounds + paste-into-app? If not, what is closest and what is missing?

### Takeaway
No app was found that combines (1) a fixed-eye, text-slides-away writing surface, (2) key sounds, and (3) output pasted into the focused app. The pieces exist separately: Smashkey/Ilys/MDWA-hardcore/Freewrite Alpha for limited visibility; OmmWriter/FocusWriter/StimuWrite/Calmly Writer/Kachunk/Thock for sounds; Wispr Flow/VoiceInk/Handy/Espanso for inserting into the frontmost app. The horizontal single-line mode is not offered by any of them.

### Cited Findings
- Closest software on visibility: Smashkey (5 lines, rolling up, no delete/arrows/selection, copy or plain-text export, no sounds mentioned) — [Gumroad](https://smashkey.gumroad.com/l/smashkey-v1); Ilys (last character only, no editing until goal) — [TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/write-drunk-edit-sober); MDWA hardcore (current letter only, per one user) — [Substack](https://eowynbolivette.substack.com/p/is-your-writing-low-quality).
- Closest hardware: Freewrite Alpha — 2–6 visible lines, mechanical keyboard with "a satisfying (but not too loud) sound", cloud sync rather than paste — [deBoer](https://freddiedeboer.substack.com/p/review-freewrite-alpha), [Liliputing](https://liliputing.com/freewrite-alpha-is-a-smaller-cheaper-distraction-free-writing-maybe-too-small-probably-too-expensive/).
- Closest on sound + writing surface: OmmWriter (ambient audio + keystroke sounds, full-screen) — [ommwriter.com](https://ommwriter.com); FocusWriter (typewriter sound, GPL-3) — [GitHub](https://github.com/gottcode/focuswriter); StimuWrite (9 typing sounds) — [itch.io](https://thatclowngoddess.itch.io/stimuwrite).
- Closest on output path: dictation apps that insert into "the app you're already using" — [Blurt](https://github.com/AssemblyAI/blurt), [Wispr docs](https://docs.wisprflow.ai/articles/7971211038-fix-text-not-pasting-after-dictation), [VoiceInk](https://github.com/Beingpax/VoiceInk).
- A floating-window-then-paste tool was searched for and not found; only scripts and ScreenFloat's direct-paste for images — [MacScripter](https://www.macscripter.net/viewtopic.php?id=47638), [Eternal Storms](https://blog.eternalstorms.at/?p=5846).

### Inferences
- What reviews say is missing, assembled across tools: (a) calm, not punitive, forward-only writing (MDWA users report panic); (b) enough context to avoid "paranoia" without inviting re-reading (deBoer: 5 lines made the Alpha usable, 2 did not); (c) backspace for typos (LessWrong reviewer, Hemingway Typewriter fork); (d) zero typing lag (Traveler's main complaint); (e) sounds scoped to writing, not system-wide (Typewriter Keyboard complaint); (f) a clean hand-off of the text into the real destination (Smashkey offers only copy/export; Freewrite syncs to cloud services).
- A horizontal single line is a stronger version of the Alpha's 2-line mode; deBoer's reaction suggests offering an optional faint previous line (or a few words of trailing context) to manage the "document disappearing" feeling.

### Gaps
- iOS/iPad-only tools (Blind Draft, Invisible Notes) were not checked for paste/share-out features.
- No App Store search for brand-new 2026 apps in this niche was possible (App Store pages often 404 or are not indexable); a manual App Store/Setapp/Product Hunt search would close this.

## 6. Real user quotes: what makes these tools work or fail

### Takeaway
Users praise forced forward motion and freedom from the inner editor; they complain about panic under deletion threats, losing the thread when they can see too little, missing backspace for typos, typing lag on hardware, sound apps firing when not writing, and clipboard/paste unreliability in insertion tools.

### Cited Findings
- Forward motion works: "That forces you to just dump out the words from your head as they are thought." / "No time for thinking either. That's a relief." / "It was fun." — [LessWrong MDWA review](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/5cJq8wGw2WACm9gHR/review-the-most-dangerous-writing-app).
- Deletion threat causes stress: "I'm panicking a bit already," "I cannot lose this all, it's a whole paragraph already!" — [LessWrong](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/5cJq8wGw2WACm9gHR/review-the-most-dangerous-writing-app). MacMyths: useful for rough drafts and brainstorming but risky for anything that must be saved — [MacMyths](https://macmyths.com/dont-stop-writing-or-your-words-will-vanish-off-the-page/).
- Backspace is needed: "I make enough typos that using backspace is a requirement anyway." — [LessWrong](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/5cJq8wGw2WACm9gHR/review-the-most-dangerous-writing-app). A no-delete typewriter-style editor "feels awful in practice" because people rely on backspace more than they expect (search summary of a Hacker News-adjacent source; primary not pinned).
- Too little context: "a certain paranoia that creeps in when you can't see anything you wrote more than a sentence or two ago" / "More lines makes me feel like the document isn't constantly disappearing into my memory." — [deBoer on Freewrite Alpha](https://freddiedeboer.substack.com/p/review-freewrite-alpha).
- Little context helps: "with the small screen, I can't see what all has been written so it can't distract me" / editing is "annoying"; workflow: "keep what I've written and edit once I upload it" — [Writing Forums AlphaSmart thread](https://www.writingforums.com/threads/alphasmart-neo-review.140507/); "the screen forced me to concentrate on the sentences I was writing" — [43 Folders](https://www.43folders.com/node/47350/317995); an Alpha owner: the small screen "forces the writer to push forward instead of worrying about going back and editing" — [Freewrite reviews page (vendor-hosted)](https://getfreewrite.com/fr/pages/reviews).
- Ergonomics of tiny screens: the Alpha's display is barely above the keyboard, so "you also may find yourself straining your neck" — [Liliputing](https://liliputing.com/freewrite-alpha-is-a-smaller-cheaper-distraction-free-writing-maybe-too-small-probably-too-expensive/).
- Lag kills it: Traveler "slight, but noticeable lag when typing" — [AppleInsider](https://appleinsider.com/articles/23/07/02/freewrite-traveler-review-portable-distraction-free-writing); vendor-page owner: lag "was the one thing they couldn't get past" until a software update — [Freewrite reviews](https://getfreewrite.com/fr/pages/reviews).
- Word count as feedback is ambivalent: "It shows me the word count. I'm not sure if that helps at all." — [LessWrong](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/5cJq8wGw2WACm9gHR/review-the-most-dangerous-writing-app).
- Sounds: positive — "it was making me want to work and type more due to how pleasant it was" — [Klack App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/klack/id6446206067); key sounds make typing "more rewarding and interactive" and the music doesn't overpower them — [Capterra OmmWriter](https://www.capterra.com/p/151933/OmmWriter/reviews/). Negative — system-wide sound on every keystroke "even when you aren't writing, such as when launching an app with Alfred" was "a little annoying" — [L&L forum](https://forum.literatureandlatte.com/t/customized-typing-sounds/15277).
- Typewriter-mode editing friction: iA recommends turning focus mode off to edit because of screen jumping — [iA support](https://ia.net/writer/support/editor/focus-mode); Scrivener "The Cursor in Typewritermode is still jumping around" — [L&L forum](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=146660).
- Insertion tools: clipboard overwritten and polluted — [Superwhisper feedback](https://superwhisper.userjot.com/board/p/restore-the-last-value-from-the-clipboard); old clipboard pasted / paste only in some apps — [Wispr docs](https://docs.wisprflow.ai/articles/7971211038-fix-text-not-pasting-after-dictation).

### Inferences
- The user-reported sweet spot is "see a sentence or two, not a page": two lines felt like the text was vanishing (deBoer), 5–6 lines felt usable (deBoer, AlphaSmart users). A single sliding line plus a faint trailing context is untested in the market and should be user-tested early.
- Calm framing matters: the panic quotes come from deletion threats, not from hidden text itself; Ilys/Smashkey/Alpha users describe hidden text as relief, not stress.

### Gaps
- Few direct Reddit (r/writing, r/macapps) or Hacker News quotes were retrievable through search; the quotes above are from blogs, forums, App Store and vendor pages. A manual Reddit/HN search would add volume.
- No user reports specifically about losing text in Flowstate or MDWA were retrieved verbatim, only descriptions of the mechanism.
