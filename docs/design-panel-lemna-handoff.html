Lemna hand-off motion design options

The user, 2026-10-29: "almost a second of delay" after ⌘⏎, and today's animation "really lackluster". A parallel pass cuts the delay; this panel designs a motion that starts within one frame of ⌘⏎ and shows the text leaving Lemna for the app behind. recommended is the proposed pick, ask a matter of taste, no ruled out by evidence.

What happens today

From the code and DESIGN.md F1 (E1, P1), 2026-10-29.

It starts late.done() waits for Return's key-up (1 s at most) before paste() hides the box; nothing moves until then. AppKit gives a key-up with ⌘ down to no window, so the panel missed it and waited the whole second. The parallel pass (17a) catches it with a monitor; the box still waits for Return.
It says "done", not "sent".A 45° white band (peak α 0.35) crosses a still picture of the line in 350 ms, the fade from 150 ms. Nothing moves toward the app.
The dim lags.undim holds the dim 150 ms, then fades it over 200: the target app clears only at 350 ms.
The pieces exist.Line.draw places each glyph with its own alpha (K1 adds scale and shift); the ghost, the curve and Effects.duration exist.

H: the motion

Over a light app, the box in today's colours (T2) with its accent border (B1), at the first frame and at 60, 120 and 220 ms.

Conflict: ease-in against frame 1.H1 and H3 use ease-in as briefed, which starts slowest: in the first 120 Hz frame the glyph at the caret moves 4 pt of its 363 (1 %), on easeOutQuint 123 pt (34 %); at 60 ms no glyph has left (computed). Omarchy: "Never an ease-in curve on UI: it starts slowest at the moment the eye is on it." For it: Material's accelerated easing for exits, no braking at the edge. Both at once: easeOutQuint aimed well past the edge, so a glyph crosses it at speed and slows once clipped. Drawn as briefed; "H1 on quint" takes the other.
H1

Send

recommended

The line leaves: from the caret leftwards each glyph sets off ~8 ms after its right neighbour and accelerates off the box's right edge (ease-in, ~220 ms in all); the empty box then shrinks to 90 % and fades over 160 ms.

  • Frame 1: the dim and the caret go at ⌘⏎; the app is in full view before a glyph has left. The siblings: "Overlays brighten at once."
  • It shows where the text went: rightwards, like text typed into the target; shrinking reads as going back into the screen.
  • The bright clause (~19 glyphs, Menlo 28, a 907 pt box) staggers over at most 120 ms; the faded rest goes with the last (computed).
  • Esc keeps P1's plain fade: a hand-off never looks like a dismissal.

Risk. 380 ms, longer than the 300 ms open; Omarchy wants exits at two thirds of the entrance. Collapsing from 120 ms ends at 280.

H2

Drop

The whole box falls an eighth of the screen with a fade, 140 ms ease-out (the terminal app's push), the dim lifting with it; the text unchanged.

  • A sibling's motion reused; the shortest; 17 % of the way at frame 1 (computed).

Risk. Down is not toward the app, which is behind. A box that sinks and fades reads as dismissed, close to Esc's fade.

H3

Fold

The box folds flat about its middle (height to 0 over 180 ms, ease-in) while the line streams out to the right as in H1; the dim lifts at once.

Risk. Two motions at once, the text squashed as it goes; a fold reads as closed, a screen switching off. 0.4 % of the fold at frame 1 (computed).

H4

Today's sheen, faster

no

No. Light passing over still text says "done", not "leaves"; it is what the user called lackluster.

A: a cue where the text arrives

Something in the target app, after the motion.

A0

None

recommended

The pasted text appearing in the app is the cue.

Risk. A slow paste leaves a gap after the motion; the parallel pass closes it.

A1

Window outline

ask

A 1 px accent outline flashes around the target window's frame, ~150 ms, at the ⌘V.

  • The frame from Accessibility, whose permission the ⌘V already has; one more overlay window.

Risk. As late as the paste; an onset at the window's edge, not where the text goes.

A2

Caret dot

no

A small accent dot at the target's caret.

No. Caret bounds through Accessibility are missing or wrong in many apps (unverified per app).

T: timing against the paste

Today as met on 2026-10-29: the key-up missed, ⌘V at 1120 ms.

T1

From the key-down

recommended

The motion starts at ⌘⏎'s key-down and never waits for the paste; a ⌘V before its end is fine. At most 400 ms.

  • Moves the start from paste() into done(), while the box still holds the keys that would auto-repeat ⌘⏎ into the app.

Risk. The box must give up the keys at Return's key-up while H1's line is still leaving (Cost).

T2

Ends at the ⌘V

no

The motion is timed to end exactly when ⌘V is posted.

No. It stretches with the delay it should hide, and needs the paste's time measured first.

4

C: Copy and Save

⌘⇧⏎ and ⌘S hand off too, to the clipboard or a file.

C1

The glyphs rise

recommended

The same send, upward: up to the clipboard. Save the same, once the sheet has written the file.

  • A ⌘⏎ that copied instead (no permission, Secure Input) rises too, and the box stays with its note.

Risk. Up as "clipboard" is a convention to learn.

C2

As the paste

Copy and Save leave right, as a paste.

Risk. A copy then looks like a paste that went nowhere.

S: the switch, and what it costs

Not a pick.

Switch.Session › Effects ▸ Hand-off replaces Hand-off sheen and drives the whole hand-off; Pop keeps the open and Esc's fade. Reduce Motion and Effects ▸ Off: the box and the dim go at once.
Where it runs.The exit: one more x term per glyph in Line.draw for 220 ms; then the ghost's shrink and fade on Core Animation. Budget: key → presented p50 / p90 no worse than the spike's 42 / 60 ms, measured with os_signpost.
Risk: the band's window.The band presents only while its window is on screen, and draws on the main thread, which at ⌘⏎ also writes the clipboard and the journal. If the panel cannot stay up without the keys, or a frame slips, the exit moves to the ghost: one layer per glyph cut from the last frame, moved off the main thread.
