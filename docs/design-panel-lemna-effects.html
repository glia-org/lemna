Lemna effects design options

Omarchy's feel comes from one sharp curve, short durations, a still accent border and a theme-coloured dim, not from motion per key. Nine decisions and a cost line: recommended is the proposed pick, ask a matter of taste, no ruled out by evidence; the argument is in the effects notes and the ambience note of 2026-10-28.

What Omarchy actually does

Read from its repository on 2026-10-28 (Omarchy note).

Nothing per keystroke.Its motion rules (2026-10-24): "A keystroke that acts and is done jumps." Terminals keep a still block cursor, Omawrite's caret is a still 1 px bar, and text effects run only in the screensaver, ending at the first key.
One curve.easeOutQuint, cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1), for every animation since 2026-10-28: no ease-in, no bounce, nothing over 300 ms.
Short.A window opens in 250 ms from 95 % and closes in 160 ms; the border colour changes in 150 ms.
Still surfaces.An accent border, the launcher's dim in the theme background at 0.5, Tokyo Night tokens; blur and shadows off by default since 2026-07-13, "a source of GPU load".

Lemna's glide is 1 − (1 − u)³, exactly cubic-bezier(1/3, 1, 2/3, 1); over the same 140 ms Omarchy's curve covers 90 % of the distance at 50 ms instead of 75 ms (computed).

F0 stays.Its verdicts hold: no glow, breathing, rhythm trace, particles, colour drift or ripple. This panel adds switchable effects on top; Effects ▸ Off is F0, and Reduce Motion turns every moving effect off.
One wording changes.The recommended defaults (P1, B1, K1, E1, E2) rewrite F0's "nothing beyond the glide and the fade"; Off restores it.
IDs.This panel's own: DESIGN.md's S1, K1, T2 or G1 are other decisions.

T: theme tokens

The box's colours, over a light app under today's dim.

T1

The siblings' Tokyo Night roles

recommended

Background #1a1b26, text #c0caf5, muted #7f88b2, caret #7aa2f7, line #3b4261.

  • the terminal app and the brief app use these roles; Tokyo Night is Omarchy's default (siblings note).
  • Muted passes the HIG's 4.5:1 at 4.9:1 without Increase Contrast; today it is 3.2:1 (computed).
  • A lighter box: over a white app the surround drops from 127 to 57 times the box's luminance (computed).

Risk. The app icon (L1) is drawn in today's colours; it keeps them or follows.

T2

Keep today's colours

#0e1013, #d8dce3, #7aa7f0, muted #5c646f.

Risk. Darker than every Omarchy dark theme but two; muted passes only under Increase Contrast.

T3

A Theme submenu

later

The siblings' schemes: Catppuccin, Nord, Gruvbox, Everforest, Kanagawa, Rose Pine, Graphite.

Risk. Each scheme needs its muted checked and every effect tried on it; no need shown yet.

2

P: box open and close

How the box arrives at the hotkey and leaves at a hand-off or Esc; the dim (D1) shown with it.

P1

Omarchy's pop

recommended

Open in 250 ms from 95 % with a fade, close in a 160 ms fade, both easeOutQuint; the dim follows the same curves.

  • Omarchy's window open and close since 2026-10-28; 90 % there at 90 ms.
  • It shows where the box came from, a few times a day; the rules Omarchy adapts forbid motion only at 100+ uses a day.

Risk. Omarchy's launcher, the nearest surface, opens at once, and its rules make keyboard dismissals jump: if Esc feels slow, Esc jumps. The system shadow does not follow a layer's scale (unverified).

P2

Instant

On and off screen at once, as today.

Risk. None; Omarchy's launcher does the same.

3

G: the glide's curve

The same 140 ms, a sharper start: how much of the new glyph is out from under the caret, per 120 Hz frame.

G1

easeOutQuint, 140 ms

recommended

Omarchy's curve over today's 140 ms: 90 % of the advance at 50 ms instead of 75 ms.

  • One line in Line.ease (a lookup table); drawn in the band as now, at no cost.
  • Brings the motion you notice into the 60–80 ms DESIGN.md left as the try, keeping the tail.
  • Always on: a curve, not an item in the Effects menu.

Risk. A sharper start may read as a jump; tried in a minute, reverted in one line.

G2

Keep the cubic

1 − (1 − u)³, as the terminal app's glide.

Risk. At 240 ms between keys the line moves 58 % of the time, its second half a slow tail (visuals.md §6.1).

4

D: the dim, and Q: the box's alpha

What surrounds the box, and how much of the app shows through it. Both over a white app, the box in T1.

D1

Theme background at 0.5

recommended

The dim takes the box's background colour at α 0.5: Omarchy's launcher scrim.

  • Over a white app the surround falls from 57 to 44 times the T1 box; today's box and dim give 127 (computed). Lighting standards ask at most 10.
  • The deeper dim DESIGN.md held as a try (H2, α 0.5–0.6).

Risk. The app you paste into shows less.

D2

Black at 0.35

Today's dim.

Risk. The bright surround raises the threshold for any small change inside the box (visuals.md §5.4).

Q: the box's alpha

Omarchy's lesson (its Glassy toggle): put alpha on the background, so text stays crisp. Lemna sets 0.97 on the whole panel, text included, and Box.swift says why: "the line's Metal band is opaque, and a background a touch lighter than it would show the band as a stripe."

Blended in sRGB at 3 % of the app behind (estimate); the stripe's strength depends on that app.

Q1

Opaque box

recommended

Alpha 1 on the whole box: text at full strength, and the box looks the same over any app.

Risk. None found; it is what Reduce Transparency does already, and at 3 % little showed through.

Q0

0.97 on the whole box

Today: text at 97 %, which Box.swift says looks the same.

Risk. The box is a shade lighter over a light app than over a dark one.

Q2

Alpha on the background only

no, with an opaque band

Omarchy's way: the background translucent, text at full strength.

No, as built. The margins turn lighter than the band, a stripe; a non-opaque band may cost the text its smoothing (unverified).

5

B: the border

The single most Omarchy element.

B1

1 px accent border, still

recommended

Omarchy's active border, in the box's accent, never changing.

  • The box is always the focused thing, which the accent means there.
  • Still, so F0's "moves while typing" does not reach it; one layer property.
  • Omarchy draws 2 px; 1 px is the siblings' line.

Risk. A second accent beside the caret; if it competes, B0.

B0

No accent

The 1 px hairline the box has today (#262b32; #3b4261 with T1).

Risk. The least Omarchy of the four.

B2

Border follows typing

ask, opt-in

B1 while keys come; over ~2 s of pause it cools to the line colour in 150 ms steps; the next key restores it.

  • Omarchy's border marks focus coming and going, not each key.

Risk. It says you stopped: the ambience note's rhythm trace in a weaker form, a mild pause cue against P0. An extra menu item, off by default.

B3

Neon gradient

ask

Hyprland's stock border, #33ccff → #00ff99 at 45°, Omarchy's Tokyo Night border until v3.2.

Risk. Decoration: the most saturated colour on screen, next to the caret.

6

K: per-key effects

Each its own menu item; several can be picked.

K1

Glyph pop-in

recommended, default on

The new glyph emerges from under the caret scaling up from 95 % and fading in over 120 ms, easeOutQuint.

  • It is what you just typed: Omarchy's exception, "Inside something the user stays and watches, keys get the same motion as the pointer", and the glide's own case.
  • No new light: it softens the onset the glyph makes anyway; 90 % opaque at 43 ms.
  • In the band, one more parameter in the per-glyph draw, during frames the glide draws anyway: no extra latency.

Risk. At 28 pt the scale is under a point; the fade is what shows. Animated text can read as lag (visuals.md §6.1).

K3

Border sheen per word

ask, opt-in

On Space, a 300 ms light sweep along the border.

Risk. A sweep is travelling motion, the class Bartram ranks most distracting, about once a second; outside the fixation point, off by default.

K2

Caret pulse

no

The caret brightens on each key and decays over 150 ms.

No. A 3–8 Hz flicker at the fixation point while typing (visuals.md, the keystroke glow).

K4

Wake

no

A faint lighter trail behind the caret, fading over 300 ms.

No. Travelling motion over the words you just wrote (Bartram 2003).

7

E: event effects

Tied to an event, not to keys; several can be picked.

E1

Hand-off sheen

recommended

At ⌘⏎ or ⌘⇧⏎ one 45° band of light crosses the box in 300 ms as it closes.

  • Omarchy's About-logo sheen, on an event: it confirms the hand-off, once per session.
  • Sheen and close overlap and end together; in a row they would hold the paste ~460 ms. The paste never waits.

Risk. Outside the letter of "A keystroke that acts and is done jumps". Untested whether the box can give up the keys while it fades; if not, it hides at once and the sheen goes.

E2

¶ fade on Return

recommended

On Return the ¶ fades in over 120 ms, opacity only.

  • The ambience note's smallest version of the ripple: no ring, no motion, drawn during the glide Return starts.

Risk. Perhaps unseen; then drop it.

E3

Idle sheen

no

After 20 s without a key a sheen crosses the line, like Omarchy's screensaver.

No. It marks the pause: pause pressure, against P0 ("stressed" was the commonest feeling, Dux Speltz 2021).

8

S: switches

Where effects are turned on and off, beside Sound.

S1

An Effects submenu

recommended

Session › Effects ▸ next to Sound, in the menu-bar item's menu and the main menu: a check item per effect, then Off.

  • Box pop, Border, Glyph pop-in, Word sheen, Hand-off sheen, Paragraph fade; Border follows typing with B2. The glide curve is not an effect.
  • Off keeps the checks, as Sound ▸ Off keeps the pack.
  • No key: effects are set once, not muted for a call like sound; ⌥⌘K beside ⌘K if one is wanted.
  • Kept in UserDefaults. Reduce Motion turns every moving item off and greys it; the still border is not motion and keeps its setting.

Risk. Seven items for small effects; S2 if you never mix.

S2

One toggle

Session › Effects as one check item: all or none.

Risk. No way to keep the border and drop the sheens.

9

C: what it costs

Not a pick: where each recommended effect runs, and the budget it answers to.

Off the main thread.P1, D1's fade, B, K3 and E1 animate properties of their own CALayers through Core Animation; the key path only adds an animation after its frame is presented.
In the band.G1, K1 and E2 change how the band draws in frames the glide draws anyway. Budget: key → presented p50 / p90 no worse than the spike's 42 / 60 ms, measured with os_signpost before it lands.
Nothing at rest.No effect loops, none runs while idle or while the box is hidden.
No blur.NSVisualEffectView has no radius control and is recomposed on every glide frame; Omarchy dropped blur for GPU load.
One switch.Every duration goes through one function that returns 0 under Reduce Motion or Off, as Omarchy's Style.duration does.
