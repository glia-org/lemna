Hypha: logo round 1

Today's menu-bar mark stays; this round iterates on it. M0 is the mark as StatusMark draws it. Eight variants each change one part and keep its half-point grid; three app icons follow from the mark. recommended is the proposed pick, a red tag a reading that fails at 16 px. M0: the mark today

16 × 16 pt template image, black on clear; the menu bar tints it. A 12 × 1.5 pt line at y 7.5, a 1.5 × 8.5 pt caret centred on it at 60 % of its width. Edges on half points.

Sharp at 2×.Every edge falls on a pixel: 0 soft pixels.
Soft on one side at 1×.On a 1× display each 1.5 pt stroke is one full pixel and one at half alpha: 19 of 38 lit pixels soft.
Close to a +.Two bars of one weight crossing near the middle: at a glance a plus sign. The variants worth having break that: by weight (M2) or by height (M8).

I: the app icon

1024 px canvas, Apple's 824 px rounded square, the app's colours: surface #0e1013, text #d8dce3, caret #7aa7f0. An accessory app has no Dock icon: this one is seen in Finder, System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility and Login Items, mostly at 16–32 px. Drawn with the recommended M2+M8 (fade, caret longer above); another M pick carries over.

I1

Mark on the surface

64 · 32 · 16 px, on a light and a dark Finder

The mark in the app's colours on its own surface: text fading in, the caret blue, the empty rest of the line a faint track.

  • Caret at 60 % of the square, fade from 25 % to 45 %: the surface's numbers (S1, V2).
  • The track keeps M0's line right of the caret, so icon and mark match.

Small. A filled part, a dim track and an upright bar: at 64 px and below it reads as a slider.

I2

The box

64 · 32 · 16 px, on a light and a dark Finder

The summoned box (W2) over a dimmed app: what the user sees when the hotkey lands.

  • Box at 60 % of the width in the lower third, the line inside it with the same numbers.
  • Three faint bars behind it stand for the app being written into.

Small. At 32 px the line in the box is under 1 px: a dark pill on grey, close to any text-field icon.

I3

Tip glow

recommended

64 · 32 · 16 px, on a light and a dark Finder

No track: nothing right of the caret, as on the surface (S1). The line's last stretch turns caret blue and a faint glow sits at the caret: the growing tip.

  • The blue of the line's end is the hypha-tip gradient; the glow is under 30 % alpha.
  • M2 without its dim rest: the square gives the 60 % that the mark's right part gives in the menu bar.

Small. The glow is gone below 64 px; small, it is a line fading into a bar, which nothing else in a Finder list looks like.

How it is madeA Swift script run with the Command Line Tools: NSImage reads icon-I<n>.svg (it reads SVG natively, gradients and clipping included; tried on this Mac). It skips SVG filters, so the script draws the drop shadow itself (setShadow), draws it at 16–1024 px into Hypha.iconset, iconutil -c icns packs it, make-app.sh copies the .icns and sets CFBundleIconFile. No Xcode, no new tool. The mark stays in code (StatusMark); its SVG is the reference.
macOS 26Tahoe puts an icon whose outline is not the system's rounded square into a grey tile. These use Apple's 824 px body as a superellipse (n = 5, within 1 px of the template's 185 px corner at 45°); to be checked on the first build.

Recommendation

M2+M8 for the mark, I3 for the icon.

  1. M2, the fade. It keeps both bars and the grid and adds the surface's own rule: text fades in toward the caret (V2), the rest of the line is empty. The caret alone at full strength is the growing tip, and the + goes, because the arms no longer weigh the same.
  2. M8 with it. The caret 1 pt higher turns the line into a baseline and the caret into a text cursor; the + goes further. Same bars, same sharpness. Without M8, M2 alone is the smaller step.
  3. I3, the tip glow. In the square nothing needs to sit right of the caret, so the icon shows the surface as S1 has it, with name.md's bright tip. Small, it is a line fading into a bar, which no other Finder icon is; I1 turns into a slider, I2 into a text field.
  4. Out: M1 (a plainer +), M3 (a wisp), M4 (a slider knob), M7 (a check mark). M5 and M6 can join later: M6 if a 1× display matters (sharp there, the only one), M5 shows at 2× only.
