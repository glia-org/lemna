Design panel · Lemna

Lemna ambience design options

Omarchy's screensaver plays terminaltexteffects over every screen while you are away and stops at the first key. Lemna turns it round: a scene plays on the dim around the box while you type, never on the line, and dies out within seconds of silence. Six decisions: recommended is the proposed pick, ask a matter of taste, later worth having after the first set, no ruled out by evidence or by the design; the argument is in the Omarchy note (§5.4, §7) and the ambience note (§0, §3).

What a scene is

Its ancestor, where it plays, what feeds it, and the evidence, once.

The ancestor.Omarchy v3 runs tte --random-effect full screen at 120 fps after 150 s idle; v4 runs ttfx, a Rust port, on a layer per display that "appears and disappears at once, exiting on input". 37 effects, all while idle.
Turned round.Typing feeds the scene, silence starves it. Each mode borrows one tte effect's motion, not its text: the dim has no characters to reveal.
Where.On the dim of the box's screen, between the shade and the box, which covers it: nothing crosses the line. Not in the window (⌃⌘F), which has no dim.
Fed by.Key times, Space, Return, ⌫, and the glyphs that leave the box's left edge. Nothing else of the text.

Every mode ends the same way: nothing new after the last key, the last particle gone within 4 s, then the link pauses and the layer is cleared.

Decided.Modes, one at a time, switchable, with Off (the user, 2026-10-28). Reduce Motion forces Off.
The evidence, once.Travelling motion beside a task is the most distracting kind measured (Bartram 2003: blink, linear, zoom, travel, from least to most; worse during a text task than during games), and continuous motion beside text editing cost the most (Maglio & Campbell 2000). No mode has a measured benefit: they are for the look, which is the reason given. Each card rates its mode on the ambience note's attention cost, 0 to 3.
F0's rejections.Particles and the Return ripple were rejected for the box (F0's d and g). Any mode amends that for the dim, opt-in: the evidence is the same, the wish is new. Off is today, F0 with F1's effects.
The pause.A scene that dies in silence shows that you stopped: a weak form of F0's rejected rhythm trace ("stressed", Dux Speltz 2021). Kept weak: nothing happens at the pause; the last particles live out their life.
IDs.This panel's own: DESIGN.md's M1, D1, R1, S1, C1 and I1 are other decisions.

M: modes

Which to build first; several can be picked, one plays at a time. Each over a dimmed dark app, with what feeds it, its tte ancestor and its rating.

M1

Drift

recommended, the name's

Glyphs that leave the box's left edge float off over the dim like duckweed on slow water, fading out over 4 s.

  • Fed by the line. V2 hides a glyph from 25 % of the width; when it crosses the box's edge, about 13 characters later (computed: 25 % of a 907 pt box at 28 pt Menlo), it fades in on the dim in 300 ms and drifts off.
  • Current 8–40 pt/s leftward, set by the key rate, with a slow wander and turn per glyph; about 20 glyphs live at 5 keys/s.
  • Ancestor none exact: tte crumble and scattered part characters, smoke drifts.

Rating 2. Slow travel, but each glyph fades in rather than appearing and moves away from the caret. The one mode made of your own words.

M2

Rain

recommended

Faint columns of glyphs fall over the dim, matrix-like; each key starts one, so typing sets the density.

  • Fed by each key: a column at a random x on the box's screen, the typed glyph at its head, a trail of 4–8 cells, a cell's glyph swapping now and then; Space and Return start none.
  • Life 2.5 s a drop, falling 150–300 pt/s: 3 s after the last key the screen is clear. About 90 glyphs live at 5 keys/s (computed).
  • Ancestor tte rain and matrix, the most screensaver-like pair.

Rating 3, the highest picked. Continuous linear travel over the whole periphery, the condition that cost editing most (Maglio & Campbell). The look asked for; kept to one drop per key and none without one.

M3

Rings

recommended

Each key sends one faint ring out from the box's edge across the dim, rounding out as it grows, gone in 1.5 s.

  • Fed by each key: a 1 pt ring, α 0.25 → 0, out to ~400 pt on F1's easeOutQuint; rings add, so fast typing reads as a shimmer, not a strobe. ⌫ sends one inward, onto the box: receding motion does not capture (Franconeri & Simons 2003).
  • About 8 rings live at 5 keys/s (computed).
  • Ancestor tte rings and waves (circle_center_to_outside); a frond on still water.

Rating 2. An expanding onset beside the box at every key, 3–8 a second. If busy, one ring per word (Space): one line.

M4

Beams

later

Light beams sweep the dim: a short one beside the box on Space, one across the screen on Return.

  • Ancestor tte beams, "beams which travel over the canvas illuminating the characters"; the dim has none, so the beam is added light, accent at α 0.18 with a white head.
  • About one beam a second at 50 wpm (computed: a word every 1.2 s).

Rating 3. Fast travel across the field, and Return's beam lands in the planning pause. Later, if Rings is not enough.

M5

Grid

recommended

A faint grid of square cells shows on the dim while you type; each key lights one cell near the box, which fades in 0.6 s.

  • Fed by each key: one cell within three of the box, more often near the caret; the grid lines, α 0.06, fade out 4 s after the last key. About 3 cells lit at 5 keys/s.
  • Ancestor tte synthgrid, "a grid of balanced, approximately square cells"; 24 pt cells.

Rating 1–2, the lowest here. Anchored pulses, Bartram's least distracting kind, and no travel; still an onset per key beside the box. The least like water.

M6

Fireworks, Spray

no

On Return a small burst of glyphs leaps above the caret and falls.

  • Ancestor tte fireworks, "launches characters up the screen where they explode", and spray.

No; rating 2–3. A looming burst at the start of a paragraph, the planning pause, where F0 turned down a far smaller ripple; once a paragraph is too rare to read as ambience.

M7

Bubbles, Swarm

no

Bubbles rise from the box's top edge with the rhythm and pop, or a swarm circles above it at the key rate.

  • Ancestor tte bubbles, "bubbles float down and pop", and swarm, "characters are grouped into swarms and move around".

No; rating 3. Continuous travel with nothing of the text in it; Drift gives the water with less motion.

M8

Burn

no

The text burns away at the line's left end in the accent colour.

  • Ancestor tte burn, "characters are ignited and burn up the screen"; the Omarchy note's e7.

No; rating 3. On the line, the one place every mode stays off: flicker inside the text 3–7° left of the caret (computed from the ambience note's geometry), where V2 quiets old words, and drawn in the band on the main thread (Omarchy note §7: "in the band: no").

D: the default

What the first launch shows; after that the pick is kept either way.

D0

Off at first

recommended, weakly

The first launch is today's box; you pick a mode once and it is kept.

  • F0 stays the evidence default: no mode has a measured benefit.
  • Personal builds only (X1), so the default is seen once.

Risk. One trip to the menu before anything moves.

D1

Drift at first

Drift plays from the first launch.

  • The calmest travelling mode, and the one tied to the name.

Risk. Motion by default on the most frequent action, which the HIG asks to avoid ("avoid adding motion to UI interactions that occur frequently").

R: rendering

Where a scene is drawn, and what drives its frames.

R1

Its own Metal layer, particles on the CPU

recommended

A clear CAMetalLayer above the shade in the dim window of the box's screen; a particle simulation stepped at 60 Hz, at most 2000 particles, one instanced draw a frame.

  • The key path appends (time, kind, glyph) to a lock-free ring after its frame is presented; the band's draw knows nothing of it. Drift's glyphs come from the line's model, not its drawing.
  • One change from the brief: not the line's display link. It runs at 120 Hz and pauses at rest; waking it for 4 s per key wakes the band's tick, which the spike tuned away. A CAMetalDisplayLink (macOS 14) on its own thread steps the scene and pauses 4 s after the last key; that it runs off the main thread on macOS is to verify.
  • Seeded and stepped by time, so a scene renders offscreen to PNG in a test, as the band does.
  • The app's first shader: with no Xcode it is compiled from source at the first scene (makeLibrary(source:)), once.

Risk. About 400 lines and a shader to keep (estimate); a full-screen layer composited while typing.

R2

Core Animation emitters

A CAEmitterLayer or shape layers per mode, birthRate pulsed per key; Core Animation simulates.

  • Least code for Rings and Grid; Rain needs an emitter cell per glyph image, Drift a CATextLayer per glyph.

Risk. The current cannot follow the key rate without replacing animations; a transaction per key on the main thread; nothing to test offscreen; GPU cost unknown (the ambience note's particles).

CPU.About 0.05–0.2 ms a frame at 2000 particles, off the main thread: 25–50 ns to step one and write its 32-byte vertex, ~50 µs to encode the draw (estimate, not measured). Nothing at rest.
GPU.2000 small quads: trivial. The full-screen layer's clear and composite, 5.9 Mpx on a 14-inch screen at 2×, ~0.35 Gpx/s at 60 Hz: small.
Power.Only while typing and 4 s after; nothing while idle or hidden; other screens keep a plain dim.
Budget.Key → presented p50 / p90 no worse than the spike's 42 / 60 ms with a scene playing, measured with os_signpost before it lands.

S: the switch

Where a mode is chosen, beside Sound and Effects.

S1

An Ambience submenu

recommended

Session › Ambience ▸ beside Sound and Effects, in the main menu and the menu-bar item's: one item per built mode, then Off; one ticked.

  • A radio list as Sound ▸ is, Off last as there; kept in UserDefaults (ambience).
  • Apart from Effects ▸ Off: each submenu has its own Off.
  • Reduce Motion greys every mode and none plays; the pick is kept.
  • No key: chosen once, not muted for a call.

Risk. A third submenu of looks.

S2

Inside Effects

The modes as a radio group in Effects ▸, under its check items.

Risk. Radio and check items in one menu, and Effects ▸ Off would have to stop both.

C: colour

What a scene is drawn in, and what the app behind does to it.

C1

The box's colours, faint

recommended

Text #d8dce3 for glyphs, rings and grid lines, accent #7aa7f0 for heads, beams and lit cells, at α 0.06–0.35 over the dim.

  • The scene reads as the box's own light spilling out; nothing new to tune beside T2.

Risk. Over a light app it nearly vanishes: a glyph at α 0.3 is 4.4:1 against a dimmed dark app and 1.1:1 against a dimmed white one (computed). Lemna cannot see the app behind without the screen-recording permission.

C2

tte's palettes per mode

ask

Each mode in its ancestor's default gradient: rain blues, matrix greens, rings violet to cream, synthgrid magenta to white.

Risk. Saturated colour beside the box, which B3's neon border was declined for; four palettes to check over light and dark apps.

Fixed per mode

recommended
