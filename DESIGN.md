# Flow (working name): design decisions

A separate macOS app, not part of the terminal app. Research and the option panel: `docs/design-panel-flow-writer.html`,
backed by `reports/Flow writing tool design options.md` and `research_notes/`. The stack spike: `spikes/`.

## Decisions (the user's picks, 2026-10-27: the panel's recommended bundle)

| ID | Decision | Pick |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | Surface | One horizontal line, caret fixed at ~60 % of the width, text glides left per keystroke (one offset for the whole line, +advance easing out over 140 ms, leftover capped at one advance); nothing is ever drawn right of the caret: the new glyph emerges from under the caret as the line glides (clipped at the caret; the user, 2026-10-27, after seeing it overlap the caret in the spike); Reduce Motion jumps |
| V2 | Visible span | Last words at full strength, the clause before fades out toward ~25 % of the width |
| B4 | Editing | **Amended 2026-10-27 after the user tried the box** ("I would like to be able to also edit words that I already typed before"): the caret moves through the text (←/→ by character, ⌥←/→ by word, ⌘←/→ to the paragraph's start/end); typing inserts at the caret; ⌫ deletes backwards without limit within the text (across spaces and paragraph ends), ⌥⌫ a word back, ⌦ / ⌥⌦ forwards; no selection, no mouse placement, no undo on the line (the review had them; it is gone since 2026-10-28, O1+O2+O3). The line keeps the caret fixed and glides the text so that the caret never moves: text before the caret as before (V2 fade to the left), text after the caret drawn too, at full strength up to 80 % of the width and fading to nothing at 95 %; an insertion pushes only the text before the caret (which keeps emerging from under it), the text after stays put; a deletion glides back. The rule "nothing right of the caret" now applies to the text before the caret only. Concern raised and overruled: hidden history is what cut corrections from 42 % to 9 % with no quality loss (Pinet & Nozari 2020), and the earlier pick B1 followed from it; the user chose full editing with that in view. Replaces B1
| P0 | Pressure | None; text is never destroyed |
| K1 | Key sound | Clicks from the app's own window only (key-down, preloaded samples, pitch variation, one audio stream); default on, one toggle; warn once on Bluetooth output |
| W2 | Window | **Amended 2026-10-27 after the user tried v1** ("it doesn't open like a small box where you can type, it's treated as a full class separate window"): the surface is a small floating box, summoned by the hotkey or the menu-bar item over whatever the user is doing: a non-activating panel (the app behind stays active), about 60 % of the screen's width, one line tall plus margins, in the lower third of the active screen, rounded, with the rest of the screen dimmed by per-screen overlays; Esc hides it, and so does a click outside it while none of Flow's own sheets is up (the session stays; pass 7b, 2026-10-27); the app is an accessory (menu-bar item, no Dock icon). The full window of v1 stays reachable (Flow › Open Window, ⌃⌘F) as an ordinary window for long sessions; tiling window managers see only that one. Replaces the W1 pick. **Amended 2026-10-28** with the review's removal (O1+O2+O3): the box no longer grows to hold a review; it stays one line tall, and a hand-off's note sits at its bottom edge
| O1+O2+O3 | Output | **Amended 2026-10-28 after the user tried the box** ("I do not want the first window after you pressed cmd+enter the first time, this is really not necessary"): no review. ⌘⏎ (Done) applies R1 and pastes at once into the app that was active when the box was summoned (O3: hide the box, reactivate that app, pasteboard + a posted ⌘V, the old clipboard restored after 250 ms if the pasteboard still holds Flow's item); needs the Accessibility (PostEvent) permission, asked once; with no permission, Secure Input on or no app to paste into (always so in window mode) it copies instead, and the box stays up with the reason in a one-line footer until the next key or Esc. Copy ⌘⇧⏎ (Concealed/Transient markers, this host only) and Save… ⌘S (Markdown to a chosen folder), from the box, the window and the menu-bar item's menu; after a paste, a Copy or a Save the box (or the window) goes. Every hand-off finishes the session at once (E2): no grace, no Undo; the next summon shows an empty line. Discard… stays in the menu-bar item, asking once. Was (O3 added 2026-10-27 with the box): Done opened a review, ⌘⏎ in it pasted or copied, and a 10 s grace kept the text in Flow (Undo = keep) before finish() |
| E2 | Never lose, privacy | Encrypted append-only journal (`lib/FlowJournal`); per-session key in the keychain, destroyed on a hand-off (paste, Copy, Save…) or Discard, at once (**amended 2026-10-28** with the review's removal, O1+O2+O3: no 10 s grace before finish()); edits batched into 50 ms records (no keystroke timing on disk), each record write()n at once and followed by F_BARRIERFSYNC, F_FULLFSYNC on ~1 s idle, deactivate, quit (an app crash loses at most the last 50 ms); ⌘Q never discards, silent restore; a flock per journal so two builds never share a file. "This device only" holds only once the data-protection keychain is used (Apple allows kSecAttrAccessible on macOS only with it). **Signing:** the user has no Developer ID (2026-10-27), so dev builds are signed with a self-signed "Flow Dev" certificate from the login keychain (`app/make-cert.sh`, 2026-10-28; ad-hoc before, which voided the Accessibility grant and the keychain item's access on every rebuild) and the key lives in the login keychain; the key store is one type, swapped for the data-protection keychain when an identity exists |
| R1+R3 | Review | Deterministic pass always; AI only as hand-off to Apple Writing Tools / Shortcuts. No in-tool LLM, no silent background fixes. **Amended 2026-10-28: Harper removed** (the user: "I think the post-fixing of error is out of scope"): R2, Harper's review per change (offline, English, a Rust static library), is gone from the app. No evidence of need: the 5–10 draft test (Open 2) never ran, and B4's full editing lets the user fix a typo on the line. The cost was ~9.5 MB of a 10.4 MB binary, ~200 crates, a Rust toolchain in every build, English (American) only. The code stays in git history (3f9fca8 `lib/FlowHarper`, 0afb32f the review's column) if it is ever wanted again. **Amended again 2026-10-28** with the review's removal (O1+O2+O3): R1 is applied silently to what Done, Copy and Save… hand off, with no toggle and no undo; nothing shows its fixes. "No silent background fixes" still holds for the text while it is written |
| T2 | Stack | **Swift + AppKit** (the user, 2026-10-27, after the spike): custom NSView with NSTextInputClient, Core Text into a CAMetalLayer, AVAudioEngine, SwiftPM with the Command Line Tools (no Xcode), a script assembles and ad-hoc signs the bundle. Why not T1 (GPUI): the spike showed equal latency (p50 42 vs 50 ms at 60 Hz, both ~2.5–3 frames), and GPUI 0.3.8 needed five workarounds (no key timestamps, no presented-time hook, Reduce Motion unread, no event injection, 30 fps inactive cap) for 139 KB vs 6.7 MB, 0 vs 360 dependencies. Other OSes later mean a UI rewrite; accepted |
| M1 | Session | **Removed 2026-10-28 after the user tried the box** ("Also I don't like the time-box or intent modes, it's not really necessary"): no time box, intent line, peripheral progress line, soft end, summary or private log; a session starts free, shows no counts, and ends at a hand-off (O1+O2+O3) or Discard…. The Sound toggle (K1) stays. Was: free start; optional time box and hidden intent line; peripheral progress line; no live counts; soft end; summary; private log (totals only, never keystroke timing) |
| X1 | Distribution | Developer ID, notarized, outside the App Store, when the user has an identity; until then personal use only |

## Spike results (2026-10-27, this Mac, 60 Hz display, 300 synthetic keys at 12/s)

| | GPUI (gpui-pre 0.3.8) | AppKit (Swift 6.2) |
|---|---|---|
| key → presented p50 / p90 | 50 / 56 ms (mostly 3 frames) | 42 / 60 ms (mostly 2.5 frames) |
| handler p50 | 0.9 ms | 11.7 ms (draws in the handler) |
| glide fps | 60.0 | 59.9 |
| stripped binary / dependencies / cold build | 6.7 MB / 360 crates / 60 s | 139 KB / 0 / 35 s |

Both wait for the display-link tick and then for vsync. Known cuts for the app: draw on the key event instead of the next tick (about half a frame), `maximumDrawableCount = 2` (one frame). ProMotion was not exercised. Dead keys not yet tried by hand.

## Plan (v1), one milestone at a time, each with tests and a try by the user

Items 1–8 built on 2026-10-27 (`app/`, `lib/FlowJournal`, `lib/FlowClicks`); item 7 removed on 2026-10-28, and the parts of items 3 and 5 marked below; the user tried the window, the box, paste and editing. `HANDOFF.md` lists what to try.

1. **Surface** (S1 V2 B1 P0, W1's window): the app bundle skeleton (`app/`, SwiftPM, `make-app.sh` assembles `Flow.app` and ad-hoc signs it), an ordinary resizable window, the line from the spike with clip, fade and glide, real paragraphs in the model (shown as `¶` on the line), dead keys, Reduce Motion, 120 Hz hold, draw-on-key. Tests: layout core, offscreen render to PNG.
2. **Journal** (E2): AES-GCM append-only journal, key store (login keychain now), write/barrier/flush policy, silent restore, Done/Discard destroys the key. Tests: round trip, kill -9 mid-session then restore, discard leaves noise.
3. **Output and the mechanical pass** (O1 O2 R1): Done → summary → copy (markers, host only) / save Markdown (atomic, chosen folder) / discard; R1 with one-line undo; shred after a confirmed hand-off with an undo grace. The summary (the review), R1's undo and the grace **removed 2026-10-28** (O1+O2+O3, R1+R3): Done pastes, Copy ⌘⇧⏎, Save… ⌘S, R1 applied silently, the session finished at once.
4. **Sound** (K1): AVAudioEngine, preloaded buffers, round-robin and pitch variation, a CC0 pack with its licence recorded in `THIRD_PARTY.md`, toggle, Bluetooth warning.
5. **Session frame and summoning** (M1, W1): hotkey (Carbon), menu-bar item, optional time box / intent line, peripheral progress line, soft end, summary, private log. The session frame (time box, intent line, progress line, soft end, summary, private log) **removed 2026-10-28** (M1); the hotkey and the menu-bar item stay.

6. **The box and paste** (W2, O3; 2026-10-27): the floating panel, dim overlays, accessory policy, paste at Done, window mode kept.
8. **Editing on the line** (B4; 2026-10-27): caret movement, insert and delete anywhere, journal edits carry positions (format v2, v1 journals still replay).
7. **Harper review** (R2), **removed 2026-10-28** (R1+R3): `lib/FlowHarper`, a Rust static library (harper-core, Apache-2.0) with a C ABI, wrapped by a Swift package; suggestions in the review, per-change accept. Built 2026-10-27, never tried; in history at 3f9fca8 and 0afb32f.

Later: the name, Developer ID.
