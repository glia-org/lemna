Lemna

Write a thought down in one go, without rereading it.

Summon it over any app with ⌃⌥Space, write forward while the text glides past the caret, and ⌘⏎ puts it into that app.

Get it on GitHubmacOS 14 or later, build it yourself for now.

  • ⌃⌥Space summons the box over the app you are in.
  • ⌘⏎ pastes into the app behind; it needs the Accessibility permission, for the paste only.
  • ⌘⇧⏎ copies, ⌘S saves, Esc hides.
  • Every key is kept in an encrypted journal on your Mac until the text has gone out.
  • A click per key from four sound packs, or off; an ambience on the dim, or off.
  • Nothing leaves your Mac: no network, no account, no telemetry.

Ambience

Optional, on the dim while you write: Drift, Rain, Rings or Grid, or off.

Keychron pack: Keychron K10 key sounds by Sadiquecat on Freesound, CC0; the clicks on this page are from it.

Soft pack: MacBook Air M2 and FUJITSU key sounds by Sadiquecat on Freesound, CC0.

Typewriter pack: Silver Typewriter (Keystroke), (Space Bar), (Carriage Return Lever 3) and (Margin Release and Carriage Return) by moodyfingers on Freesound, CC0.

Electric pack: typewriter_electric_typing_01.wav, typewriter_electric_spacebar.wav, typewriter_electric_spacebar_several_times.wav and typewriter_electric_return.wav by magedu, from the pack Typewriter, licensed under CC BY 4.0; changed for Lemna: cut into single key sounds, high-pass filtered at 150 Hz, peak-normalised, some slowed and lowered in level, the return also spliced shorter.
