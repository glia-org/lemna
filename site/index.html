Lemna

A quiet place to write without stopping.

Press ⌃⌥Space over any app, write for as long as you like while the text glides past the caret, and ⌘⏎ pastes all of it into that app.

DownloadmacOS 14 or later. Lemna is not notarized by Apple: the first time, macOS blocks it; then click Open Anyway in System Settings › Privacy & Security. Source on GitHub.

  • ⌃⌥Space shows the box over the app you are in; you can choose another shortcut in Settings.
  • ⌘⏎ pastes the text into that app. macOS asks once for Accessibility permission, which Lemna uses for the paste alone.
  • ⇧⌘⏎ copies it instead, ⌘S saves it as a Markdown file, Esc hides the box and keeps the text for next time.
  • If the paste lands nowhere, the text is still on the clipboard, and ⌘Z in the empty box brings it back.
  • Until you paste, copy or save it, your text is saved as you type, encrypted, on your Mac, so a crash loses nothing; then Lemna deletes that copy.
  • Two themes: Still, the default, paper-coloured by day and warm dark by night, as your Mac’s appearance is; and Lemna, dark and monospaced.
  • Key sounds from five packs, a background sound (rain, ship, lab, space or synth) and an animation on the dimmed screen, each of them optional. Lemna starts with the lab background sound and the Code animation.
  • An option in Settings makes other apps play quieter and softer while you write. macOS asks once for permission to record system audio; nothing is recorded or kept.
  • Nothing leaves your Mac: no network, no account, no telemetry.
